Questions clinics ask

Is this medical advice?

No. Gentle Recovery Kitchen offers general food ideas only. It does not replace your clinic's instructions, and every page tells patients to follow their dentist's or oral surgeon's advice. It is meant to sit alongside your own aftercare sheet.

Who writes the content?

All content is created and maintained by Neural Conclave. Your Clinic Edition lets you share it with your patients under your own branding.

Can we change the content?

The Clinic Edition covers branding: logo, colour and contact details. If you need the day ranges or wording adapted to your own protocol, email us and we'll quote for a custom edition.

What does "unlimited patient copies" mean?

You may print or email the guide to as many of your own patients as you like. It is yours to keep. It is for your patients only, not for resale or for other clinics.

Do you offer other languages?

Yes. Both the 94-page English edition (Gentle Recovery Kitchen) and the 96-page Dutch edition (De Zachte Herstelkeuken) are illustrated, set in 12pt type, and included in the Clinic Edition.

Is this a subscription?

No. The Clinic Edition is a one-off purchase of EUR299. The branded booklet is yours to keep.

Can we see it with our own logo first?

Yes. Email us your clinic name and website and we'll send a free branded sample.
